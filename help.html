HB Durability HP Guide

Glossary Back to app

What it does

Rates the surface durability (pitting) horsepower of a first-reduction stub tooth herringbone gearset on standard or extended centers per AGMA 211.02A (Aug. 1966), at six standard pinion/gear BHN combinations (295/255 … 390/350).

  • C1 = RPM × PDPE² × Cv / 126000, with PDPE = ODP − 2×(0.8/DPT) and Cv = 78/(78+√v), v = π·RPM·PDPE/12
  • C2 = −0.0153·FW² + 0.7969·FW − 0.0193 (face-width factor)
  • C3 = K × (Sac/Cp)², with (Sac/Cp)² = ((25600 + 345·BHNG)/2300)² and K the geometry factor from operating pressure angle, ratio, contact length Z and base pitch
  • C4 = 1.0   ·   Pac = C1×C2×C3×C4   ·   Service HP = Pac / SF

Practice

1. Spreadsheet example. 83 RPM, FW 6, DPT 2, 24/132 teeth, ODs 13/67, CE 39, PAT 25°, HA 0, SF 1.5. Check: Pac at 295/255 BHN ≈ 195.48 HP, service HP ≈ 130.32. Ask Grok
2. Service factor. Electric motor, moderate shock, 24 hrs/day → click 1.50 in the table. What is the service HP at 390/350? (≈ 216.38) Ask Grok
3. Extended centers. Change CE from 39 to 39.25. Watch RC, PATE, contact length and Pac change. Ask Grok
4. Face width. Raise FW from 6 to 8 and see C2 rise. Ask Grok

Glossary

Abbreviations used in the app's inputs, results and this guide. Lengths are in inches unless noted; angles in degrees.

Inputs

INPUT RPM / RPM
Revolutions per minute of the pinion (input shaft). Enter the pinion speed, rev/min.
FW
Face width: the axial width of the gear teeth in contact (total across both helices of the herringbone), in.
DPT
Diametral pitch, transverse: teeth per inch of pitch diameter, measured in the plane of rotation, 1/in.
NP / NG
Number of teeth on the pinion / on the gear.
ODP / ODG
Outside diameter of the pinion / of the gear, in.
CE
Center distance, extended (operating): the actual center distance the set runs on, in. Equal to C on standard centers.
PAT
Pressure angle, transverse: the cutting (generating) pressure angle in the plane of rotation, °.
HA
Helix angle of the teeth, °. 0 gives spur-gear geometry.
SF
Service factor: a divisor (from the tables, by prime mover, load character and hours/day) that reduces the calculated rating to a service rating. Dimensionless.

Results & geometry

OUTPUT RPM
Gear (output) speed = RPM / gear ratio, rev/min.
GEAR RATIO
NG / NP.
C
Standard center distance = (NP + NG) / (2·DPT), in.
RC
Ratio of centers = CE / C. 1.000 means standard centers; above 1 means extended centers.
DPN
Diametral pitch, normal = DPT / cos HA, 1/in.
MN / MT
Module, normal / transverse = 25.4 / DPN and 25.4 / DPT, mm.
PAN
Pressure angle, normal, °. The sheet computes atan(tan PAT × cos HA); the app keeps that formula.
A
Addendum = 0.8 / DPT (stub tooth), in.
PDPE
Pitch diameter of the pinion, as used here = ODP − 2·A, in.
v
Pitch-line velocity = π·RPM·PDPE / 12, ft/min (fpm).
Cv
Dynamic (velocity) factor = 78 / (78 + √v). Dimensionless.
BDP / BDG
Base circle diameter of the pinion / gear = (N / DPT)·cos PAT, in.
PATE
Pressure angle, transverse, at the extended (operating) center = acos(cos PAT / RC), °.
SINPATE / COSPATE
Sine and cosine of PATE.
TANHA / COSHA
Tangent and cosine of HA.
ZP / ZG
Distance along the line of action from the pinion / gear base-circle tangent point to its outside diameter = √((OD/2)² − (BD/2)²), in.
ZL
Length of the line of action between the two base-circle tangent points = CE × SINPATE, in.
Z
Length of contact (line of action) = ZP + ZG − ZL, in.
BPT
Base pitch, transverse = (π / DPT)·cos PAT, in.
BPN
Base pitch, normal, in (sheet formula: π·cos PAT / DPT × sin PAN / sin PAT).
Transverse contact ratio
Z / BPT: average number of teeth in contact in the plane of rotation.
Overlap ratio
Face (axial) contact ratio = FW·tan HA·DPT / (2π), as in the sheet. Total contact ratio is the sum shown in the sheet.
K
Geometry factor for surface durability = (COSPATE·SINPATE/2)·(ratio/(ratio+1))·(0.95·Z / BPN). Dimensionless.
C1
Speed/size factor = RPM·PDPE²·Cv / 126000.
C2
Face-width factor = −0.0153·FW² + 0.7969·FW − 0.0193.
C3
Material factor = K × (Sac/Cp)².
C4
Fixed at 1.0 in the sheet.
BHN, BHN-P / BHN-G
Brinell hardness number of the pinion / gear. The app rates six standard pairs; only the gear BHN enters the formula (as in the sheet).
Sac
Allowable contact stress = 25600 + 345·BHNG, psi.
Cp
Elastic coefficient (AGMA), √psi. Fixed at 2300 for a steel pinion on a steel gear; not a user input.
Pac
Allowable (calculated) durability horsepower = C1×C2×C3×C4, HP.
HP / SERVICE HP
Horsepower. Service HP = Pac / SF, the rating to compare against the actual load.

Related terms in other Ram-Gear/Blake programs

Not used in this app. From R.C. Blake Company, Explanation of the Farrel Formula (Farrel manual #610A, pp. 45–46), and Using the Farrel Formula to Convert Comparator Readings to CTTT @ PDE & CTTT @ PD.

PDE (PDPE, PDGE)
Pitch diameter at the extended (operating) center, pinion and gear. For a single gear PDE = PD.
CTTT / CTTN
Circular tooth thickness, transverse / normal, at PDE, in. CTTT @ PDE = PDE·((π/2 + 2·TANPAT·DPT·X)/N + INVPAT − INVPATE). In Farrel's printed form, tte = De[(1.5708 + 0.72794·X·Pd)/N + 0.014904 − Be], where 1.5708 = π/2, 0.72794 = 2·tan PAT and 0.014904 = INVPAT (both for PAT = 20°), De = PDE, Pd = DPT, N = NP or NG, and Be = INVPATE. For a single gear, CTTT @ PD = PD·(CTTT @ PDE / PDE − INVPAT + INVPATE).
X
Comparator offset in the CTTT formula: X = ((OD − PD)/2) − AB − CR, in, where OD is ODP or ODG, PD is PDP or PDG, AB is the addendum of the comparator block (0.8/DPT for stub-tooth herringbone sets) and CR is the comparator reading (CRP or CRG). Example from the Farrel CR sheet: ODP 14.16, PDP 13.2, AB 0.32, CRP −0.004 gives XP = 0.164 in.
CPT / CPN
Circular pitch, transverse / normal, at PDE. CPT = CTTTP + CTTTG + BLCT (with BL = 0, just the sum of the two tooth thicknesses); CPN = CTTNP + CTTNG + BLCN.
BL, BLCT, BLCN
Backlash; circular backlash transverse; circular backlash normal (taken as feeler-gauge backlash). Set backlash isn't always split equally between gear and pinion.
INV (INVPAT, INVPATE)
Involute function of the pressure angle (inv φ = tan φ − φ, radians) at PAT and at PATE. For a single gear INVPAT = INVPATE.
CR, CRP / CRG
Comparator reading, for the pinion / gear. Oilfield herringbone drawings usually list only CRP, ODP, CRG, ODG, PAT, HA and CE.
B&S
Chordal tooth dimensions (programs like “B&S to Span & CR” convert chordal dimensions to span and comparator readings).
MOB
Measurement over balls, converted by the “MOB to CR…” programs to comparator readings, chordal and span dimensions.
CA, CT
Not defined in this reference; see the program that uses them.
Notes: the "ACTUAL BHN's" column in the original sheet (401 … 255) is reference data only and not used in any formula. Cp is fixed at 2300 (steel/steel).
